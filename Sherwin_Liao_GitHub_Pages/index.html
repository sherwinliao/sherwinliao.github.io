About

My work begins with a simple tension: an AI system may appear to speak for medicine, while its actions are shaped by a platform, an institution, and an interface.

I am a Master of Global Media Communication graduate from the University of Melbourne, with a strong foundation in critical discourse analysis, platform studies, AI governance, Chinese media research, and qualitative analysis. Building on this media and communication foundation, I am extending my research toward human-centred approaches to understanding how people interpret, navigate, and oversee AI systems.

Current question When AI health systems and services interpret information, make recommendations, route users through care pathways, and hand them over between services, how can people understand who is acting, on what authority, and where human control should intervene?
